What each course expects

The Foundation is designed around roughly three hours of engaged learning per course. You are not graded for sitting in a chair; you advance by doing the work and meeting the academic standard.

Learn

Read the approved course material and use the workbook as the working surface for the ideas.

Practice

Use low-stakes workbook work and Professor Grey before the protected assessment. Practice is for learning, not for padding a grade.

Submit

When course work calls for a workbook response, practice, reflection, or project, save it to your Registrar record.

Check readiness

Grey can tell you what looks solid, what needs another pass, and give you a fresh practice problem without exposing a test question.

Assess

Official assessment is separate from teaching. A course is not complete until its protected assessment clears the published 90% standard.

Take the time you need

About three engaged hours per course is the planning target. Faster or slower is fine; mastery and completion, not a stopwatch, control advancement.

Student support

Access and academic review

Real structure also means telling students what happens when the format gets in the way or when a recorded result needs another look.

Access / accommodation

If a course format, device limitation, reading presentation, timing issue, or other access barrier is stopping you from doing the work, contact New Beansland at founder@newbeansland.org. NBLU can review format, timing, or workflow adjustments case by case where technically feasible without lowering the academic standard or exposing protected assessment material.

Do not send passwords, payment-card data, or unnecessary medical details in an access request.

Request academic review

If you believe a recorded course result misunderstood your reasoning, you can put a review request directly into your Registrar record.

Academic review is not an automatic retake and does not guarantee a changed result. If an unusual answer is already pending final review, you do not need to submit another request.

Academic rules

What counts here

NBL University evaluates reasoning, not confidence, status, polished wording, or guessing what the instructor wants to hear.

90% standard

Completion is earned under the published Foundation passing standard.

Practice is for learning

Use Grey, the workbook, reflections, and practice work before entering protected assessment.

Protected means protected

Grey may teach the concept but will not reveal, reconstruct, hint toward, or coach official answers, rubrics, keys, or future questions.

Review can stay unresolved

An unusual answer may be held for academic review instead of being forced into a fake score.

Your record persists

LOCKE and the Registrar rail preserve enrollment, submissions, progress, attempts, results, and completion state.
