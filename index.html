What about contracts, music rights and ownership?

We make the process visible without posting unsigned blank master contracts as if they were a finished deal. A project-specific agreement should describe the services, deliverables, price, payment schedule, credits, permissions and any agreed licensing or rights transfers.

Creators keep the pre-existing work they legally own. NBL keeps its own pre-existing work. Rights in a newly commissioned beat, soundtrack or finished production—including any royalties or buyout—depend on the written agreement for that project.

If a song uses someone else's unlicensed beat or samples, we may discuss a genuinely original or properly licensed alternative. Simply modifying a protected recording does not automatically clear its underlying rights.

This is a general overview, not a contract or an offer of a specific rate. No rights are transferred, no project is accepted and no fee is charged just by opening this page.
